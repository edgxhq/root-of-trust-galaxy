Bitcoin has no owner and no admin key was found. Its blocks are made by miners who work through pools, and
three pools assemble more than half of them. Nearly all nodes run one codebase and its derivative, merged by a
handful of maintainers funded by several organisations.

Its rules change by proposal and by what miners and node operators choose to run. There is no on-chain record
of those decisions, so the smallest deciding group cannot be counted.
