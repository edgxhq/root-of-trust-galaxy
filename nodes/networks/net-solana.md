Fourteen operators hold a third of Solana's stake, the share that can halt the chain; counted one validator at a
time it is eighteen ([[m-solana-block-production]]). Most stake runs forks
of one client, whose code is approved by one company's teams; a second, independent client holds a growing
share. Protocol changes are accepted by core contributors and switched on feature by feature.

The network halted about seven times between December 2020 and February 2024 and has not halted since.
