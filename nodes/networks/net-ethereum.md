Ethereum has about ten client teams, more than any other network here. The trackers disagree on shares, and
by some of them one client sits near half of each layer.

No owner and no admin key was found. Its rules change through public proposals and developer calls, with
nothing recorded on-chain until a fork activates.

Most of this network's links have not yet been counted for this research.
