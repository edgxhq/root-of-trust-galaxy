An XRP Ledger server trusts the validators on its list, and by default uses two lists: one from the XRP
Ledger Foundation and one from Ripple. Rule changes need more than four fifths of trusted validators for two
weeks, and the result is recorded on the ledger.

So its rule changes are recorded, which is more than can be said for some larger networks, and they rest on
lists that two organisations publish.
