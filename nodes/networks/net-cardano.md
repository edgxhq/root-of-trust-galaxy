Cardano's blocks are made by stake pools chosen in proportion to the stake delegated to them. Since 2024 its
rules and its treasury have been governed by on-chain votes of delegated representatives, stake pools and a
constitutional committee, and the keys its founders held were destroyed.

By the shortest-stave test its strongest links are who makes the blocks and how the rules are set. Its
shortest is the diversity of its node software: one implementation makes the blocks, with others close to production. Who employs
the people able to merge that implementation has not been counted.

On the Edinburgh index, which measures five layers and does not count node implementations, Cardano has the
highest lowest layer of the four chains scored on all five.
