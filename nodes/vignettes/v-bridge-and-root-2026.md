In July 2026 a third-party bridge between Cardano and another chain was exploited, and a large amount of bridged
NIGHT was taken. The token's price fell to its low.

Charles Hoskinson's reassurance that day was exact: "none of Midnight's infrastructure has been hacked, and nothing
on the Cardano side related to Midnight has been hacked".

**What it cost what was built on it.** Whoever held NIGHT through the bridge held a claim on something that was
gone.

**What it shows.** Both lessons in one event. What sat on the bridge was only as safe as the bridge. What sat on
the root was fine.

The second half of the quotation, from "nothing on the Cardano side", was checked by ear on 25 November 2026; the
first half is from automatic captions. The size and cause of the exploit are from press reports.
