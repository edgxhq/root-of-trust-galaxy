A trade report in October 2025 describes Bhutan "positioning itself as the first country to anchor a nationwide
identity framework on a public blockchain". "Rather than placing personal data on-chain, the system anchors
verifiable credentials to Ethereum".

**What it shows.** Governments are starting to pick public roots of trust for records that matter, and the
choice is not automatic. A root is chosen for what it offers at the time. Being the best candidate has to be
earned and shown.
