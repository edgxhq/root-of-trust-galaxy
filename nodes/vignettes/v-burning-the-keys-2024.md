Until 2024, keys held by three founding organisations under a five-of-seven threshold "were the only keys that
could submit protocol-parameter updates and initiate hard forks". "The keys were burned on August 30, 2024, and
on-chain CIP-1694 governance replaced them."

**What it shows.** A root changing who can change it. The standing power was real, it was removed, and what
replaced it is a recorded vote that can be counted.
