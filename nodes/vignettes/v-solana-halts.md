Between December 2020 and 6 February 2024 Solana halted about seven times; counts vary with the definition. Each
time, every application on it stopped until validators restarted in step.

It has not halted since, and a study in late 2026 recorded two years without a mainnet outage. A second,
independent client now runs a growing share of stake.

**What it shows.** With one implementation, one bug reaches every node at once. The repair is the same one every
network needs: a second implementation that fails differently.
