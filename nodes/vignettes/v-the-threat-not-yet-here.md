A large enough quantum computer would break the signatures most ledgers use. The defence is known in outline:
move to signatures that resist it. The hard part is that this is a rule change, and for coins whose owners never
act, a decision about property.

**What it shows.** An emerging threat tests the property that separates a public root of trust from a merely
durable one: whether it can change its own rules in time, by a process its users can see. A root that cannot
evolve is safe only against the threats known when it was made.

A seed for research. To gather: the proposals under discussion for Bitcoin, the plans stated for Cardano, and
how long each network's decision process has taken for past changes.
