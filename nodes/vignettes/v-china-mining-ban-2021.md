In 2021 the country hosting the largest share of Bitcoin's mining moved to shut it down. Within weeks about half
of the network's computing power went offline. Blocks slowed. The protocol's own difficulty adjustment brought
them back to pace, and within months the machines were running in other countries.

**What it shows.** A state removed half the block producers and could not stop or change the record. The
"which countries" stave was short, it was tested, and the design absorbed it.

From memory of public reporting. Dates and the size of the drop need a primary source before use.
