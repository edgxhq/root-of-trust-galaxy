A cross-chain bridge carried assets for applications on several ledgers. In July 2023 more than US$100 million
left its contracts. The team then said that its chief executive had been detained weeks earlier and that the
servers holding the bridge's key shares were under his control alone.

**What it cost what was built on it.** Applications on at least one ledger had relied on the bridge's versions
of major assets. Their value there fell with the bridge.

**What it shows.** A system described as secured by many parties can have one person at the bottom of it.
