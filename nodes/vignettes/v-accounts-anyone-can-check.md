The Cardano Foundation publishes its financial records on Cardano, and reports doing so for two years running. A
cooperative in Germany began a pilot in July 2026 recording payment flows the same way.

**What it shows.** The plainest use of a root: a record that its own author cannot quietly revise. Nobody
reading it needs to hold a token.

The examples are small and mostly reported by the foundation itself.
