In December 2022 a severe winter storm crossed North America. Miners in the region with the most Bitcoin mining
switched off to ease the load on the grid, and roughly a third of the network's computing power went quiet for
several days. Blocks came more slowly and the ledger kept going.

**What it shows.** A natural event is a test of geography and hosting, not of cryptography. A root spread across
regions slows down. A root in one place stops.
