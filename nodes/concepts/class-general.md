A ledger that runs programs anyone can deploy. Cardano, Ethereum and Solana are of this type. A loan, an exchange, a register or a vote can be built on the
root itself, with nothing in between.

A root is placed here by one test: its measurement for [[dim-programs]] is "general programs". It says nothing
about how safe the root is; that is the shortest-stave test. More features also mean more that can fail.
