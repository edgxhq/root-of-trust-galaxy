A ledger focused on money, by design: it holds and moves its own coin, with scripts kept deliberately limited.

A root is placed here when it does not run general programs and other assets are not enforced by the ledger.
Doing one thing is a strength in a root of trust. It also means that anything else is built on another ledger
and reached through a bridge: see [[distance-from-the-root]].
