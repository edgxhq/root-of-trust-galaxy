Beside a connected system's distance from the root sits a second question: is its tie to the root a rule or a setting?

A **rule** is fixed in the protocol and cannot be changed by the connected system's operators alone. A
**setting** is a parameter they can move.

The difference matters most for ties that look strong today. A share of block production reserved for the
root's stake pools is a strong tie. If the share is a parameter that the connected chain's own governance can
set to any value, it is a strong tie held by a setting.
