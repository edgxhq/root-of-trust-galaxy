A timestamping service founded on the work of Stuart Haber and Scott Stornetta has, by one press account,
published a hash of its records in a classified notice in the New York Times every week since 1995. Anyone
with a copy of the paper can check that a record existed by that date.

It is a public root of trust built from the most widely witnessed medium available at the time. Bitcoin's
design paper cites three of Haber and Stornetta's papers.

The service has an owner; the newspaper is what makes the record hard to change.
