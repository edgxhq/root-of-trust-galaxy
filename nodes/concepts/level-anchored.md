**Anchored.** The root witnesses the record.

What the root still guarantees if everyone in between fails or colludes: you can prove what the record said. Cheating can be shown, though not prevented.

Today: records published on the ledger by an institution sit here. A committee bridge proposed in Midnight's own process would be a tie of this kind.
