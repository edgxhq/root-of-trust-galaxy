Announced on 30 September 2026: "Cardano provides the traceable record" behind a scheme for sustainable
aviation fuel, "showing where an environmental benefit originated, who it was assigned to, and that it has
been claimed only once".

The useful property is the last one. A public record that cannot be quietly changed is what stops the same
benefit being claimed twice. The ledger witnesses the claim; it does not check the fuel.

The account is the Cardano Foundation's own and was not checked on the chain for this research.
