A count, for one subsystem of a network, of the smallest group that together exceeds a threshold, usually one
half. It is named after Bitcoin's pseudonymous author and was proposed in 2017 by Balaji Srinivasan and Leland
Lee, who also suggested taking the minimum across subsystems. Their essay has not been opened for this
research.

The count depends on how entities are grouped. Two fair groupings of the same network can give 14 and 56.
