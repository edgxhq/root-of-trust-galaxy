The Cardano Foundation publishes its own financial records on Cardano with a tool called Reeve, and reports
having done so for two years running. In July 2026 a German cooperative announced a pilot recording payment
flows the same way.

The ledger does not make the accounts true. It makes them impossible to change quietly afterwards, which is
what "anchored" means: the root witnesses the record.

The examples are real and small, and most are reported by the foundation itself.
