A root is publicly owned when its public holds the decisions that steer it. The test has three parts, read
from measurements in this graph, and a root must pass all three:

- changes to its rules are decided by votes recorded on the ledger ([[dim-rule-changes]]);
- payment for its upkeep is decided by votes recorded on the ledger ([[dim-funding]]);
- no one holds a special key or list over it ([[dim-standing-keys]]).

Today one root passes: Cardano. Its rule changes need recorded votes from its delegated representatives, its
stake pools and its Constitutional Committee, its upkeep is paid from a treasury by recorded vote, and its
founding keys were destroyed in 2024.

This is a test of who decides. It is not a claim that the root has no weak links. Cardano's shortest staves
are the diversity of its node software and who can merge that software's code, and both are shown on the map
like anyone else's. Any root that passes the test joins the centre.
