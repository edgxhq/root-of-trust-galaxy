Announced on 31 August 2026: Cardano is "the public proof layer in Blockforce's traceability platform", which
the announcement says has anchored more than 500,000 supply chain records. "Records stay on a permissioned
network and only their cryptographic proof reaches Cardano, so auditors can confirm a record is genuine
without seeing the underlying data."

That is "anchored" exactly: the root witnesses the record and cannot be asked to change it. It does not make
the record true, and the records themselves are kept by the platform.

The figures are the Cardano Foundation's own and were not checked on the chain for this research.
