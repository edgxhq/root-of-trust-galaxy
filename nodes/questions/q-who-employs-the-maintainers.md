Merge rights are the hardest link to count. Repositories name teams, not employers. Bitcoin's maintainers
are funded by several organisations; Solana's main client is approved by one company's teams; for Cardano,
Ethereum and the XRP Ledger the employers were not resolved.

To answer: team membership from each organisation, each member's employer from public profiles, and a count
of independent employers needed for a merge.
