Cardano's hard fork needs three bodies. Counting the most concentrated body alone understates how hard
capture is; counting the smallest coalition across all three needs a rule for adding parties of different
kinds. The same problem appears wherever control means passing in one place and blocking in another.

Until there is a rule, the measurement states the body it counted and the full threshold beside it.
