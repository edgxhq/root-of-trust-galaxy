Changes are proposed in public, merged by a handful of maintainers, and take effect only if miners and node
operators run them. Nobody owns the process and no admin key was found. There is also no on-chain record of who
decided, so the smallest group cannot be counted the way a recorded vote can.

The activation mechanics were not checked for this note.
