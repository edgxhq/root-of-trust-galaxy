Until the Conway era, keys held by the three founding organisations under a five-of-seven threshold "were the only
keys that could submit protocol-parameter updates and initiate hard forks". "The keys were burned on August 30,
2024, and on-chain CIP-1694 governance replaced them."

What remains is a Constitutional Committee of seven with fixed terms, which votes on-chain on whether an action
is constitutional. It is a recorded, replaceable body, not a standing key.
