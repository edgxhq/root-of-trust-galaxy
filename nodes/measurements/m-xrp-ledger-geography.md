Of 1,289 nodes in the public crawl on 19 November 2026, 173 had a country: United States 50, France 27,
Germany 18, Finland 14, Singapore 11. Three countries make up more than half of those.

Only about one node in eight could be located, and these are nodes, not the validators on the default list. Treat
the figure as a first reading.
