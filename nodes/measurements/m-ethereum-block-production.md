A September 2026 study by ARK Invest and Glassnode reports that Bitcoin and Ethereum each cross their thresholds
with about three entities. Lido held 21.2% of staked ETH at 30 June 2026 by a press report.

A second concentration sits beside this one. Most blocks are assembled by specialist builders before a validator
proposes them, and one unverified report puts a single builder at about half of such blocks in October 2026. If
that is confirmed the count would be lower.
