The published plan for who produces Midnight's blocks. Section 5: "the role of producing blocks will require
that prospective Midnight block producers be Cardano SPOs", "selected in proportion to their delegated ADA stake".
At launch block production is "performed exclusively by a set of trusted permissioned nodes, with a view to
progressively decentralize, open up, and become fully permissionless".

Under "Expanding network reach: multichain" (page 24 of the PDF): "Beyond the multi-resource consensus system that
allows Midnight to tap into Cardano's robust proof-of-stake layer to secure the network while bootstrapping, the
Cardano Partner Chain framework brings other features to enable the vision of a truly multichain future:
cross-chain observability and multichain signatures." Read again on 23 November 2026 in a copy of version 1.81.
