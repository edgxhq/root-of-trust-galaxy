Collects trackers of which client software Ethereum's validators and nodes run. On 19 November 2026 its sources
disagreed: one put Lighthouse at 50.78% of the consensus layer, another put Teku at 53.86%; an execution-layer
source the site itself flags as unreliable put Geth at 50.13%.
