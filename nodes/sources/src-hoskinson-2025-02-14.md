Multi-resource consensus put forward for Cardano itself. At [00:02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H4dqe9G7KJY&t=142s):
"we want to have not just proof of stake but we also want to have different algorithms running in parallel with proof
of stake", and IOG "created a protocol called Minotaur" as "a generic framework" for it. At
[00:03:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H4dqe9G7KJY&t=181s): "what happens after Leios, where do we go? Well, if we
moved in this particular direction, then what you could do is engineer Cardano to have multiple resources", and "it
actually makes the network more inclusive, meaning more people get to participate in consensus, and more resilient". He gives the reason
at [00:03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H4dqe9G7KJY&t=215s): if proof of stake were attacked, "what about the hash
power or something else". The passage at 00:03:01 was checked by ear on 25 November 2026. The others are from the
video's automatic captions, which write Minotaur as "minur", and have not been checked by ear. A direction he
describes, not a published plan.
