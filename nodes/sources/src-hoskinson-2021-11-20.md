What a root of trust is for, and what it can carry. At [00:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=saiZefjF4PM&t=288s),
once a root of trust is established: "you can use that for the rest of time to establish truth in the world. Property
ledgers for nation states, voting systems, new currencies". At [00:05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=saiZefjF4PM&t=324s), describing
what Bitcoin's supporters believe it solved: "this big decentralized root of trust, one canonical ordering for the
events of the world, the monies of the world, one version of truth that everybody can deterministically verify
themselves". The sentence before the first passage is not one to quote. Both passages were checked by ear on 25
November 2026.
