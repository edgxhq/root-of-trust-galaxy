Intersect is the member-based organisation that coordinates Cardano's development. Its weekly update of 22
November 2026, read on 24 November:

- "Five node implementations have now produced blocks on mainnet: cardano-node, Amaru, Dingo, cardano-bean and
  TSUNAGI."
- "Dingo passing a 100 Mainnet blocks milestone this week".
- A conformance issue found through contacts made at the Node Diversity Workshop in London, on 12 and 13 November,
  "is being fixed in node 11.2.3".
- For mainnet, "11.1.3 remains the recommended version", a release of the Haskell cardano-node, while a version
  11.2.x is prepared.

It gives no block counts for Amaru, cardano-bean or TSUNAGI.

Its update of 15 November (#137) put the background this way: "For most of Cardano's history, there has been one
main implementation, the Haskell node."
