A report of a Fidelity study of Solana. It gives client shares by stake (two Jito configurations about 63%
together, Firedancer configurations about 15%), says one third of stake is "concentrated across approximately 18
validators, two countries", and records "no reported mainnet outage across two consecutive years". It quotes the
study: "Adoption of two client configurations did not truly eliminate reliance on common Agave code".
