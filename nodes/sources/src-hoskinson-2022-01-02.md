The order of construction, in his words. At [00:34:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt4Sg6rs80Q&t=2072s), of what Ouroboros does: "It builds a very strong
root of trust. You end up getting thousands of, in this case 3,000 SPOs, and that's just only going to increase". At
[00:35:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt4Sg6rs80Q&t=2150s): "you have to start with a strong root of trust first, then you use that to create a subset, you use the
subset then to create a side chain". At [00:37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yt4Sg6rs80Q&t=2251s), of computation layers: "you can have many of them, and they
are just subsets of the SPOs". The passage at 00:34:32 was checked by ear on 25 November 2026. The two after it are
from the video's automatic captions and have not been checked by ear.
