The account of Cardano's founding keys and their end. Until the Conway era, keys "under a 5-of-7 threshold across
the three genesis entities" "were the only keys that could submit protocol-parameter updates and initiate hard
forks"; "the keys were burned on August 30, 2024, and on-chain CIP-1694 governance replaced them".
