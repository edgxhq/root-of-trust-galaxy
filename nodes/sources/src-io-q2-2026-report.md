Input Output's report on its treasury-funded engineering for April to June 2026, read in full on 18 and 20
November 2026. On Acropolis: "As announced on April 7, IO halted further development of Acropolis and Tiered
Pricing", returning the unspent funding to the treasury. Before it stopped the project had delivered "a Data
Node offering read-only data access". "The Acropolis codebase remains open source".

On what was to come: the Leios test network "progresses through its Water and Fire phases, with a mainnet hard
fork targeted for later in 2026", and "Nested transactions (Babel fees)" were targeted for a hard fork in the
third quarter of 2026. Neither had happened by 19 November 2026, when the chain was still in the Conway era.
