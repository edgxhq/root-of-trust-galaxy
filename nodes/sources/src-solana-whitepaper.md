Solana's founding paper, by Anatoly Yakovenko, version 0.8.14, read in the solana-labs/whitepaper repository at
commit 3c49947 (January 2019). Its title names what it is for: "A new architecture for a high performance
blockchain". It proposes Proof of History, "a proof for verifying order and passage of time between events".
