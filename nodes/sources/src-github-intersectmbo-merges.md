Read on 20 November 2026 through GitHub's own interface for programs. For six repositories, every pull request
merged from 20 November 2025 to 19 November 2026, with the person who merged it: [cardano-node](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-node/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (228),
[cardano-ledger](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-ledger/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (490), [ouroboros-consensus](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (455), [ouroboros-network](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-network/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (146),
[plutus](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/plutus/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (421) and [cardano-api](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-api/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2025-11-20..2026-11-19) (266). That is 2,006 changes, merged by 50 people. Each name opens
that repository's list of the changes merged in the year; the source's own link opens all six together.
Employers are as the people state them on their public GitHub profiles; about two in five changes were merged
by people who state none.

It shows who did merge, which is close to who can. The repositories' review rules and the membership of the
teams that own them are not public.
