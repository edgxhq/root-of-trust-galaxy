A news item by the Cardano Foundation on cardano.org, read on 20 November 2026: "Cardano is now the public
proof layer in Blockforce's traceability platform, which has anchored more than 500,000 supply chain records
for Brazil's largest fashion groups. Records stay on a permissioned network and only their cryptographic proof
reaches Cardano, so auditors can confirm a record is genuine without seeing the underlying data." The figures
are the foundation's own and were not checked on the chain for this research.
