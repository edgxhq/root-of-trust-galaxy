A report of the September 2025 Cardano R&D session, read on 22 November 2026. Aggelos Kiayias "explained how
Ouroboros Omega represents the protocol's 'final form'". The first guiding principle is adaptivity, which "Current
projects such as Ouroboros Peras ... and Ouroboros Leios ... already apply". "The second guiding principle is
multi-resource security, which extends Ouroboros beyond pure proof of stake by diversifying the resources securing
the protocol, reducing vulnerabilities from overreliance on stake alone", with "useful computational work" and
"reputation signals tied to digital identity" as examples. The page gives no date for multi-resource security.
