How partner chains are meant to be secured, described before Midnight launched. At [00:03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n3ZlObM6Lo&t=233s), the balance
of who secures one is "completely up to the person who's launching the partner chain". At [00:04:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n3ZlObM6Lo&t=285s),
with another network as the example: "we can just cut off the Cardano side and it can go its own direction". At
[00:06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n3ZlObM6Lo&t=382s), Midnight is "the first case study". The design he names is multi-resource consensus. At [00:02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n3ZlObM6Lo&t=156s): "the
Control Function is Minotaur ... this idea of multi-resource consensus", proof of stake from Cardano's delegates "but you also
could do stuff like proof of work". At [00:04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n3ZlObM6Lo&t=276s), with Algorand as the
example: "we'll just have a cardano network be a backbone under Minotaur and Algorand can take another side of it so maybe
it's a 50 50 split". The passages at 00:03:53, 00:04:45 and 00:06:22 were checked by ear on 25 November 2026. The
two at 00:02:36 and 00:04:36 are from the video's automatic captions and have not been checked by ear.
