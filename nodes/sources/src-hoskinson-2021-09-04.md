Where the phrase is applied to Cardano by its founder. At [00:29:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVA54yAaLC8&t=1775s): "the point is that you want to establish a
stable root of trust. That's what we've done here". At [00:21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVA54yAaLC8&t=1287s): "one of the really cool things about having a
root of trust is this is the single most expensive thing to establish". At [00:35:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVA54yAaLC8&t=2103s): "we have a strong root of
trust, which means we can instantiate many solutions and encapsulate them, and if those new solutions fail they
don't blow back and destroy the main chain". In the same talk he speaks of a root of trust as something each chain establishes for itself. At
[00:40:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVA54yAaLC8&t=2445s): "the single hardest thing to do, and this is what Bitcoin
solved, ... is the establishment of a root of trust", and of Cardano, "an efficient root of trust". The passages at
00:21:27, 00:29:35 and 00:40:45 were checked by ear on 25 November 2026. The one at 00:35:03 is from the video's
automatic captions and has not been checked by ear.
