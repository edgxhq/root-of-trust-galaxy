A community-run query layer over Cardano's ledger. Read on 18 and 19 November 2026 at epoch 668 for protocol
parameters, pool operator groups, DRep voting power, the Constitutional Committee and governance actions. Its
operator groups rest on labels kept by the community and are one fair way of grouping pools, not the only one.

Read again on 20 November 2026 for the size and transaction count of every block in epoch 667 (21,517 blocks), and
for the protocol parameters that set the size of a block.
