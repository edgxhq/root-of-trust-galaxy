DefiLlama's list of protocols, read through its public interface on 20 November 2026. It listed 83
applications and services with value on Cardano. Among those that run on Cardano alone, the largest by value
held were Minswap, Dano Finance and SundaeSwap (exchanges), Liqwid and Surf Lending (lending) and Djed (a
stablecoin). Centralised exchanges were among the largest holders of value overall. DefiLlama is an
independent index; its figures are not checked here and none is used.
