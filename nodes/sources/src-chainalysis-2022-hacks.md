The best-sourced figure on where thefts happen. Of US$3.1 billion stolen from decentralised finance protocols in
2022, "64% came from cross-chain bridge protocols specifically". That is 64% of the decentralised finance subset,
about half of everything stolen that year, and 2022 was the peak.
