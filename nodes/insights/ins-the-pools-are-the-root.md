Cardano's founder described its consensus in 2022 as something that "builds a very strong root of trust. You
end up getting thousands of, in this case 3,000 SPOs", and layers above as "subsets of the SPOs". The first
quotation was checked by ear on 25 November 2026; the second is from automatic captions.

In epoch 667, in November 2026, 939 pools made blocks, down from 1,134 in epoch 350, in July 2022. The protocol
aims for 500 pools of equal size, so the fall is not an emergency. It is the number to watch, and the target has
never been put to a vote.

There is also a job only operators can do: running the other node implementations, now making their first
mainnet blocks. And if
partner chains pay the root for the trust they draw on, running a pool becomes what it was meant to be, paid
work keeping public infrastructure honest.
