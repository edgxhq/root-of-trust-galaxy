On 13 November 2026 Cardano's founder said "the road map is done" and "once Leios is in, the last parts of what we're
responsible for are over", adding that "The road map is not being abandoned": dozens of firms are taking parts of it
over. Input Output's account of Ouroboros Omega, the protocol's planned "final form", makes multi-resource security,
"diversifying the resources securing the protocol, reducing vulnerabilities from overreliance on stake alone", one of
its two guiding principles, with no date given. On 14 February 2025 the founder had named it as where Cardano could go
after Leios.

So the part of the plan that would secure the root with more than stake is on no dated schedule and, by the founder's
account, not Input Output's to finish. This research reads it as a part of the roadmap for the wider community to carry,
and to put first. The founder's words "once Leios is in, the last parts of what we're responsible for are over" were
checked by ear on 25 November 2026; his other words here are from automatic captions. The Omega page was read directly
on 22 November 2026.
