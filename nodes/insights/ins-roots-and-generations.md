Cardano's founder describes Midnight as "a fourth generation cryptocurrency". On that count Cardano is third.
Generations are a fair way to track what the technology can do: programs, scale, privacy. They are the wrong measure
for a root. A root is judged by whether it is fit for the job, publicly owned, decentralised and secured as well as it
can be, and its worth grows with use. The founder said of a root of trust in 2021 that once you have one, "you can use
that for the rest of time to establish truth in the world". Calling a root a past generation risks treating it as
superseded. The quotations were checked by ear on 25 November 2026.
