On the links counted from February to November 2026 (each measurement carries its date), Cardano ranks highest or
joint highest on five of the eight, taking each network's higher figure where two fair counts disagree: block
production (56 operator groups at the higher count), merge rights (7), funding (20; the only network whose upkeep is
decided by recorded vote, so the only one counted), countries (3, joint with the XRP Ledger) and standing keys (none,
joint with Bitcoin and Ethereum).

It is behind on two: rule changes, where the XRP Ledger's validators count 25 against Cardano's 10, and hosting, where
Bitcoin counts 11 against 6. On the lower figures its lead is narrower: Solana (30) and the XRP Ledger (28) count more
block producers than Cardano's 14, and Bitcoin more people merging changes (5 against 2).

Every network scores 1, and for each the stave at 1 is the diversity of its node software ([[ins-shortest-stave]]). Several of the gaps
on the other networks are this research's, not theirs: not yet measured, rather than decided off the record.
