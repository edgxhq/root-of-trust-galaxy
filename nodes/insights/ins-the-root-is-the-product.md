This is offered as a suggestion, not a finding.

Applications, tokens and prices come and go. What they stand on is a record that anyone can check and no small
group can change. Perhaps that is the thing a public ledger makes, and it is the hardest part to make.

Cardano's founder called a root of trust "the single most expensive thing to establish" in 2021, and said of
Cardano: "the point is that you want to establish a stable root of trust. That's what we've done here".

If the root is the product, the measures change. Beside value locked and transactions sit two others: how
many people and institutions rely on the root, and how hard it is to capture.

The two kinds of measure reward different things. Value locked, transactions and the number of chains a network
connects to count what flows through it. A root is measured by what rests on it. Value that comes in over a bridge
can leave the same way, and while it is there it carries the bridge's risk: in July 2026 a bridge carrying NIGHT to
another chain was compromised, and the NIGHT held on Cardano was untouched. What is rooted in a ledger draws its
guarantees from that ledger, so it has a reason to stay, and the more that is anchored in a root, the more reason
there is to anchor there too. The same growth cuts the other way: the more that rests on a root, the larger the
prize for capturing it. That is why the second measure, how hard the root is to capture, has to grow with the first.
