On Cardano a hard fork needs 60% of delegated representative stake, 51% of stake pool stake and the
Constitutional Committee, all recorded on-chain, and treasury spending needs 67% of representative stake and
the committee.

The XRP Ledger records its rule changes too: listed validators vote and the result is on the ledger. Holders
do not vote, and no treasury is spent by vote. Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana decide rule changes off-chain.

Recorded does not mean better decided. It means the decision can be audited, and the concentration of voting
power can be seen: ten representatives hold half of Cardano's.

The Cardano side was checked directly. The other networks' processes were read by a research agent or not
yet checked.
